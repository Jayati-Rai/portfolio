Automated Vetan Bill Generator

A Python tool that fills in salary grant sheets for aided schools in Uttar Pradesh, from 15–40 minutes by hand to under 30 seconds.

Problem
Preparing each salary grant sheet in Excel by hand takes 15–40 minutes and is easy to get wrong.
What I built
An automation that fills the Excel templates while keeping formulas, merged cells, formatting and macro-enabled workbooks intact.
Result
Under 30 seconds per sheet. This is the estimate documented in the repository.

Python, OpenPyXL, Excel VBA, Flask
